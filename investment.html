Investment Management

Quwwat Group, a builder company, manages real estate investment from the first drawing to the finished building.

Built by your own builder

Invest alongside the people who build

Quwwat Group is a builder. We design, construct and deliver our own projects, so the people managing your investment are the same people on site, controlling cost, quality and schedule.

Our Investment Management service brings investors into those projects with a clear structure, one point of contact and regular reporting from groundbreaking to handover. Mountain Courtyard, our destination wedding centre at Nellankandi, is the project currently on show.

  • Projects built and managed in-house by Quwwat Group
  • Single point of contact for every investor
  • Regular progress reporting against plan and budget

Good to know

Common questions

Are returns guaranteed?

No. Every investment carries risk and the value of an asset can go down as well as up. We do not promise returns; we explain the structure and the risks so you can decide.

Who builds and manages the project?

Quwwat Group, as the builder and developer, runs construction and management with its own team.

Can I visit the site first?

Yes. Contact us to arrange a visit, or explore the interactive 3D model of Mountain Courtyard on the home page beforehand.

How do I get started?

Send us an enquiry and a member of the team will contact you to arrange an introduction.
