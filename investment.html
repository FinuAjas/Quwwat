Investment Management

Structured, transparent stewardship of community and hospitality assets, built for the long term.

The opportunity

A destination people come back to

Mountain Courtyard brings a convention centre, swimming pool, amphitheatre, children’s play area and prayer hall together in one landscaped valley setting, with parking and two entrances built in from the start.

Investment Management is how Quwwat Group looks after that place over time: operating it, maintaining it and reporting on it openly to the people who back it.

  • Mixed-use site: events, leisure and community under one management
  • Single point of contact for every investor
  • Clear, regular reporting on how the asset is performing

How we manage

Four principles behind every decision

What you can expect from us as the manager of the asset.

Careful stewardship

Open reporting

Common questions

Are returns guaranteed?

No. Every investment carries risk and the value of an asset can go down as well as up. We do not promise returns; we explain the structure and the risks so you can decide.

Quwwat Group’s management team looks after operations, maintenance and reporting for the site.
