About Us

Quwwat Group builds places where nature, recreation and community sit side by side.

Our story

A courtyard in the hills

Mountain Courtyard is a place for gathering: a convention centre at its heart, surrounded by lawns, a pool, an amphitheatre and play spaces, all set in a forested valley above Nellankandi.

We designed the site around the land rather than against it. Roads follow the slope, buildings sit within the trees, and every space is within a short walk of the next.
