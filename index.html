About the project

A destination wedding centre in the hills

Mountain Courtyard is a destination wedding centre by Quwwat Group, set in a forested valley above Nellankandi. The ceremony, the celebration and the guests' stay come together in one landscaped setting.

A convention centre anchors the site for the main event. Around it sit lawns, an infinity-edge pool, an open-air amphitheatre and a children's play area, with a quiet prayer hall on the upper slope. Roads follow the land, buildings sit among the trees, and two gateways with landscaped parking keep arrival simple.

  • Convention centre for weddings, banquets and celebrations
  • Pool, amphitheatre and play area for guests of every age
  • Prayer hall, lawns and garden paths within a short walk
